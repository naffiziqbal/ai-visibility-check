    Why your app looks empty to AI

    Lovable, Bolt and v0 build single-page apps. The server sends a nearly empty HTML file, and JavaScript fills in the page in the visitor's browser. People see your full site. But a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript only gets the empty file.

    Google does run JavaScript, though later and less reliably. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and most link-preview bots (LinkedIn, Slack, WhatsApp) mostly don't. As more people search by asking an AI, an empty page means you're not part of the answer.

    What this checks

    • Text in the raw HTML: the words a non-JavaScript crawler can read.
    • Title and meta description: what search results and AI answers show.
    • Share tags (og:image, og:title, twitter:card), including Lovable's default share image.
    • robots.txt: whether AI crawlers like GPTBot or ClaudeBot are blocked.
    • sitemap.xml and canonical URL: whether crawlers can find all your pages.
